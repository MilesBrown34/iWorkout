Week 1: Menu Rotation A

Sausage & Beans, Baked Granola + Fresh Steaks & Salads
Week A
TimeMealProtein
12:00 PM Bulk Lunch: Sausage, Black Beans & Rice (1 of 5 Servings) Sunday Batch (2.5 lbs sausage + 3 cans beans + 2.5 cups rice) → ~60g protein/container
▶ Cooking Instructions & Ingredients

Ingredients (5-Serving Sunday Batch)

  • 2.5 lbs (40 oz) homemade sausage links (sliced into coins)
  • 3 cans (15 oz each) black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 2.5 cups dry jasmine or basmati rice (cooked in bone broth)
  • 1 yellow onion (diced) + 4 cloves garlic (minced)
  • Seasonings: 1 tbsp ground cumin, 1 tbsp dried oregano, 2 bay leaves, salt & black pepper to taste

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Sear Sausage: In a large Dutch oven or pot over medium-high heat, brown sausage coins until caramelized (~8 min). Transfer sausage to a plate, leaving ~1 tbsp drippings in the pot.
  2. Sauté Aromatics: Add diced onion and minced garlic to the pot. Sauté for 3 minutes until fragrant and translucent.
  3. Cook Rice in Broth: Add 2.5 cups dry rice, 4.5 cups chicken or beef bone broth, cumin, oregano, and bay leaves. Bring to a rapid boil, cover with a tight lid, reduce heat to low, and simmer for 15 minutes.
  4. Combine & Steam: Remove lid, discard bay leaves, and fold in the drained black beans and browned sausage. Cover and let sit off heat for 5 minutes to steam through.
  5. Portion: Divide evenly across 5 glass meal-prep containers (~60g protein each). Refrigerate for Mon–Fri lunches. Reheat in microwave for 90–120 seconds.
💡 Flavor Hack: Splash each container with fresh lime juice, chopped cilantro, or your favorite hot sauce after microwaving.
 ~60g
3:30 PM Bridge Snack: Baked PB Protein Granola Clusters Sheet-pan baked clusters (oats, whey, peanut butter, bananas) — ~45g protein
▶ Baking Instructions & Ingredients

Ingredients (5-Day Batch)

  • 2.5 cups old-fashioned rolled oats
  • 3 scoops vanilla or chocolate whey protein powder (~75g protein)
  • 1/2 cup creamy natural peanut butter (melted slightly so it pours)
  • 2 medium ripe bananas (mashed thoroughly into a smooth purée)
  • 1/4 cup chia seeds or ground flaxseed + 2 tbsp honey or maple syrup
  • 1 tsp cinnamon + 1/2 tsp sea salt + 2 tbsp water (if dough is too stiff)

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Preheat: Set oven to 325°F (165°C). Line a large rimmed baking sheet with parchment paper.
  2. Wet Mix: In a large mixing bowl, whisk mashed bananas, melted peanut butter, honey, and cinnamon until smooth and creamy.
  3. Fold Dry: Add oats, whey protein, seeds, and sea salt. Stir thoroughly with a stiff spatula until a thick, clumpy, cohesive dough forms.
  4. Spread: Spread onto the baking sheet in rough 1/2-inch clusters, pressing slightly so they bind together.
  5. Bake: Bake at 325°F for 18–20 minutes until golden brown on the edges.
  6. Cool Undisturbed: Let the sheet cool completely on the counter for 35 minutes. Do not touch while warm! As it cools, the PB and caramelized sugars harden into crispy clusters.
💡 Serving: Scoop 1 cup of clusters into a bowl, or sprinkle over 1 cup of non-fat Greek yogurt for a ~45–50g protein sundae.
 ~45g
6:15 PM Dinner: Loaded Chicken Quesadillas OR Steak & Salad Lift Days: 2 Loaded Chicken Quesadillas | Rest Days: 8–10 oz Steak + Bagged Salad Kit
▶ Dinner Cooking Instructions

Option A: High-Protein Shredded Chicken Quesadillas (Lift Days)

  • 2 large low-carb / high-fiber tortillas (Mission Carb Balance / Extreme Wellness)
  • 7 oz cooked shredded chicken breast (prepped in slow cooker with salsa)
  • 1.5 oz shredded pepper jack or Mexican blend cheese
  • 2 tbsp chunky salsa + 3 tbsp plain non-fat Greek yogurt ("sour cream" sub)
  1. Heat a dry skillet over medium heat. Place one tortilla flat.
  2. Layer half the cheese, warm shredded chicken, and remaining cheese. Top with the second tortilla (or fold in half).
  3. Cook 3–4 minutes per side until the tortilla is golden, crispy, and the cheese is fully melted.
  4. Slice into wedges and serve with salsa and Greek yogurt for dipping (~65g protein).

Option B: Cast-Iron Steak & Bagged Salad (Rest/Drumming Days)

  • 8–10 oz NY Strip, Top Sirloin, or Ribeye steak
  • 1 full bagged salad kit (Caesar, Greek, or Southwest)
  • 1 medium sweet potato + coarse salt, black pepper, 1 tsp olive oil
  1. Poke holes in sweet potato with a fork; microwave 5–6 minutes until soft.
  2. Pat steak dry and season aggressively with coarse salt and cracked black pepper.
  3. Heat cast-iron skillet on high heat until smoking. Add 1 tsp oil, sear steak 3–4 minutes per side for medium-rare (130°F internal).
  4. Rest steak on cutting board for 5 minutes before slicing against the grain. Toss salad kit and serve.
 ~65–70g

12:00 PM Bulk Lunch: High-Protein Beef & Bean Chili (1 of 5 Servings) Sunday Batch (2.5–3 lbs 90/10 beef + 4 cans beans + tomatoes) → ~62g protein/container
▶ Cooking Instructions & Ingredients

Ingredients (5-Serving Sunday Batch)

  • 2.5 to 3 lbs 90/10 lean ground beef
  • 2 cans (15 oz each) black beans + 2 cans dark red kidney beans (drained)
  • One 28 oz can crushed tomatoes + one 14 oz can fire-roasted diced tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste + 1.5 cups beef bone broth
  • 1 yellow onion (diced) + 4 cloves garlic (minced)
  • Chili Spice Blend: 3 tbsp chili powder, 1 tbsp ground cumin, 1 tbsp smoked paprika, 1 tsp garlic powder, 1 tsp onion powder, salt & pepper to taste

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Brown the Beef: In a large stockpot over medium-high heat, brown the ground beef, breaking it into small crumbles (~8–10 min). Drain excess fat if needed.
  2. Bloom Spices: Stir in the diced onion, minced garlic, tomato paste, and chili spice blend. Cook for 2 minutes until aromatic.
  3. Add Liquids & Beans: Pour in crushed tomatoes, diced tomatoes, bone broth, and all drained beans. Stir thoroughly.
  4. Simmer: Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover with a lid, and simmer gently for 30 minutes, stirring occasionally so the bottom doesn't stick.
  5. Portion: Ladle evenly into 5 glass meal-prep containers (~62g protein each). Keep refrigerated for Mon–Fri. Reheat for 2 minutes in the microwave.
💡 Serving Idea: Serve over 1/2 cup jasmine rice or with 1 warm low-carb tortilla for dipping.
 ~62g
3:30 PM Bridge Snack: Baked Chewy PB Oat Protein Bars (1 of 5 Bars) Sunday Batch (pressed into 8x8 pan, sliced into 5 bars) — ~42g protein/bar
▶ Baking Instructions & Ingredients

Ingredients (5-Bar Batch)

  • 2.5 cups old-fashioned rolled oats
  • 3.5 scoops vanilla or chocolate whey protein powder (~85g protein)
  • 1/2 cup creamy natural peanut butter (melted slightly)
  • 2 medium ripe bananas (mashed thoroughly into smooth purée)
  • 1/4 cup chia or flax seeds + 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp cinnamon + 1/2 tsp sea salt + 3 tbsp almond milk or water

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Prep Pan: Preheat oven to 325°F (165°C). Line an 8x8-inch baking dish with parchment paper with an overhang.
  2. Mix: Whisk mashed bananas, melted peanut butter, honey, and cinnamon. Fold in oats, whey protein, seeds, salt, and liquid. Mix into a thick, uniform dough.
  3. Press Firmly: Transfer dough to the pan. Press down firmly with the back of a spatula or flat glass until smooth and tightly packed.
  4. Bake: Bake at 325°F for 18–20 minutes until golden on the edges.
  5. Cool & Slice: Cool in the pan completely (or chill in the fridge for 30 minutes for a dense chew). Lift out and slice into 5 large bars. Wrap individually for grab-and-go snacks.
💡 Quick Pairing: Eat 1 bar at 3:30 PM with a tall glass of ice water or a crisp apple. Tastes like a bakery flapjack with ~42g protein!
 ~42g
6:15 PM Dinner: Hearty Stew OR Steak & Salad Lift Days: Chicken & Veggie Stew (bone broth) + 2 eggs | Rest Days: 8–10 oz Steak + Salad
▶ Dinner Cooking Instructions

Option A: Hearty Chicken & Root Veggie Stew (Bulk 4–5 Serving Batch)

  • 2.5 to 3 lbs chicken breasts or boneless skinless chicken thighs (thighs stay ultra-moist in soups)
  • Two 32-oz cartons (8 cups) store-bought chicken broth OR bone broth (Swanson, Pacific, Trader Joe's, Kettle & Fire)
  • 1.5 lbs Yukon Gold or Baby Red potatoes (cut into 1-inch chunks; waxy potatoes hold up without getting mushy)
  • 4 large carrots (sliced thick) + 4 stalks celery (sliced) + 1 yellow onion (diced) + 4 cloves garlic
  • Seasonings: 1 tbsp Italian seasoning, 1 tsp dried thyme, 2 bay leaves, coarse salt & black pepper
  1. Brown the Chicken: In a large stockpot over medium-high heat with 1 tbsp olive oil, sear chicken pieces for 5–6 min until golden. Transfer chicken to a plate.
  2. Sauté Aromatics: In the same pot, cook the onion, garlic, carrots, and celery for 3–4 minutes until fragrant.
  3. Simmer Broth & Potatoes: Pour in both 32-oz cartons of store-bought broth. Add potato chunks, bay leaves, thyme, salt, and pepper. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low and simmer for 15 minutes.
  4. Combine & Finish: Return chicken to the pot. Simmer gently for 10–12 minutes until potatoes are fork-tender and chicken is cooked through. Discard bay leaves.
  5. Portion: Ladle evenly into 4–5 large glass containers (~55–65g protein each). Keeps in the fridge all week. Reheats in 2 minutes in the microwave.
💡 Broth vs. Bone Broth: Standard store-bought chicken broth works great. But if you buy store-bought Chicken Bone Broth (sold in the same 32-oz cartons), the liquid adds an extra ~75–80g of protein and natural collagen for joint health at 40!

Option B: Cast-Iron Steak & Loaded Salad (Rest/Drumming Days)

  • 8–10 oz Sirloin, Strip, or Ribeye steak
  • 1 full bagged salad kit (Caesar, Greek, or Bacon Ranch)
  • 1 cup roasted baby potatoes or sweet potato
  1. Heavily season steak with kosher salt and coarse black pepper.
  2. Sear in smoking cast iron skillet for 3–4 minutes per side until crust forms.
  3. Rest 5 minutes, slice against grain, and serve with freshly tossed bagged salad kit.
 ~65–70g
